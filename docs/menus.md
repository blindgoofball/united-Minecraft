## Container menus and Creative

Chests, furnaces, crafting tables, anvils, brewing stands, enchanting tables, the
Recipe Book, your own inventory and the Creative inventory use the game's own keyboard
navigation and narration, with a few additions:

- **Letters** jump to the next slot in the same group whose item name starts with that
  letter, wrapping around, so repeating it cycles through every match. A letter with
  Ctrl or Alt held is left to the game, so Alt+Q (drop one item), Ctrl+Q (drop the
  stack) and Ctrl+F (swap with the off-hand) still work. E does not close these screens; Escape does.
- Stepping to a slot that reads the same as the last one still says it, so you know you
  moved, and a slot is said again when what is in it changes while you are on it.

## Advancements

Each tab of the advancements screen is a tree, and the keys walk it like one. Opening
the screen narrates the current tab and how many of its advancements are done.

- **Up/Down** move through the tab from top to bottom, every branch open.
- **Right** enters an advancement's first child; **Left** returns to its parent.
- **Home/End** jump to the first or last advancement.
- **Tab / Shift+Tab** change tab, narrating its name and progress.

Each stop narrates the title (plus Challenge or Goal where it is one), whether it is
completed or its progress so far, the description, and its place in the list. The
screen scrolls to it and shows its usual hover box, so a sighted helper sees the same
thing. Advancements that are hidden until earned stay out of the list until you earn
them. The arrows and Tab rebind in the Keybindings screen, under Inventory Screens.
